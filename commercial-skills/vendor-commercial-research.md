# Vendor and Commercial Research

**Use when:** evaluating a product, service, supplier, or partner for a
business decision — e.g. choosing software, sourcing a supplier, or
assessing a new market opportunity.

## Prompt

```text
Act as a commercial research analyst.

Assess the project using:
- Market and customer need
- Competitor positioning
- Price and unit economics
- Supplier or partner reliability
- Legal, privacy, and operational risk
- Implementation difficulty
- Revenue opportunity
- Measurable next steps

Deliver:
- Executive summary
- Market findings
- Option comparison
- Risks and assumptions
- 30/60/90-day action plan
- Evidence log
```

## Suggested data sources (primary first)

- Vendor/supplier official sites, pricing pages, and contract terms
- Independent review platforms and analyst reports for validation
- Regulatory or compliance sources relevant to the industry
- Direct outreach or reference checks where feasible
